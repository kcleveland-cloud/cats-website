NASA TRACERS

Tandem Reconnection and Cusp Electrodynamics Reconnaissance Satellites

TRACERS is a NASA Small Explorer mission. Two identical spacecraft fly in low Earth orbit to study how the solar wind couples to Earth's magnetic field in the polar cusp.

Under contract to the University of Iowa, the mission's lead institution, CATS provided the Project Director. He was responsible for executing a PI-led, cost-capped mission through development, propulsion, SpaceX launch vehicle integration, and launch operations.

Digital Engineering & Analytics

Tools for faster decisions

CATS pairs engineering judgment with its own analysis tools, so Government teams get technical insight early, before major program commitments.

CATSIM™

A cloud-based CubeSat mission simulation environment for early-phase formulation. It supports rapid feasibility checks, orbit, power, and thermal sensitivity studies, and architecture trades before Milestone A, without stitching together separate modeling tools.

Decision Resistance™ Framework

An AI-enabled decision support method that finds where acquisition decisions stall. It diagnoses stakeholder alignment gaps, quantifies drivers of schedule delay, and structures trade and milestone decisions, with dashboards for program offices through senior leadership.

Defense research

CATS pursues Department of Defense SBIR research in auditable AI decision management and collaborative counter-swarm defense, applying mission assurance discipline to autonomy and uncrewed systems.

UAS Development

CATS Micro-1

A modular, additively manufactured micro-UAS that CATS is developing to prove a fast, low-cost path from design to flight hardware.

Repairable by design

Ducted quadcopter demonstrator · in prototype development

The Micro-1 is a ducted quadcopter in a 4-inch footprint, built from separate printed parts rather than a single frame. Each motor and duct pod bolts to a central electronics deck, so a damaged pod can be replaced in the field without reprinting the aircraft. Forward and downward cameras are selected from the controller.

The program follows the same discipline CATS applies to spacecraft: a controlled configuration baseline, independent design reviews, and fit checks before hardware is committed. A commercial-parts prototype proves the airframe and integration process, and a larger follow-on configuration has been defined around NDAA-aligned flight computer, datalink, and camera components.
